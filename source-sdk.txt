Metamod:Source and SourceMod are built against the Source SDKs of the games they
support (https://github.com/alliedmodders/hl2sdk, a branch per game, from the
hl2sdk package), and link their tier1, mathlib and interfaces libraries, built
from the SDKs' sources, into their game binaries.
