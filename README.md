# 🐱 leothecat – Your Terminal Cat Companion

A feature-rich terminal cat companion with 25+ moods, achievements, and interactive commands!

## ✨ Features

- **25+ Unique Moods** – Happy, Hungry, Sleepy, Zoomies, Chill, Hyper, Sassy, Ninja, Alien, and more!
- **Interactive Commands** – Feed, play, pet, train, dance, snuggle, explore, and more!
- **Trick Skill System** – Train Leo to perform better tricks!
- **Achievements** – Unlock 15+ achievements as you bond with Leo
- **Daily Bonus** – Get +5 to all stats every day you interact
- **Random Events** – Surprise encounters keep things fresh
- **Persistence** – All stats saved to `~/.config/leothecat/state.json`
- **Easter Eggs** – Rename to "Garfield" or "Nyan" for special moods!
- **Beautiful UI** – Colored ASCII art, progress bars, mood descriptions
