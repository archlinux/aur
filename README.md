# Yixee

> Memories live on — with Yixee.

[![AUR Version](https://img.shields.io/aur/version/yixee-bin)](https://aur.archlinux.org/packages/yixee-bin)

**Yixee** is a lightweight, reliable image hosting app that makes uploading, storing, and sharing visuals effortless.

Whether you're a designer, developer, blogger, or simply want a safe place for your photos — Yixee is your go-to solution. With one-tap uploads, smart compression, and cloud-based backups, your images are always in good hands.
