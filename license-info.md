The MeshCore companion app "MeshCore" by Liam Cottle (https://liamcottle.com/), available for download at https://files.liamcottle.net/MeshCore/, is closed source.

This is not an official Arch Linux package.

Email exchange with the author on 2026-02-21 confirmed:

* License is "proprietary",
* there's no EULA at this time,
* "But you're welcome to package and share it if you want. Just make sure to note that it's not an official package."

So this is kind of "freeware" with all rights reserved to the author.

Included are some libraries with open source license; their licenses can be read in `NOTICES.Z`.
